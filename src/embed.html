Before the first video

slopTV plays each post in X's own embedded player, shown unmodified inside this window. That means this window loads scripts and media from X (platform.twitter.com, x.com and X's CDNs) and X may set cookies in this app's own private browser profile (not your normal browser profile). slopTV asks X not to personalise from your activity (dnt=true), but it does not control what X's player does.

Autoplay may need one click on the video.

Keys

l / → next   h / ← previous   s shuffle   o open post on X   ? help   q quit

Keys work when this bottom bar (not the video) has focus: click it, or press Tab. Once you click inside X's player, the player keeps the keyboard.
