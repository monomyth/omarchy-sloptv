Before the first video

slopTV plays each post in X's own embedded player, shown unmodified inside this window. That means this window loads scripts and media from X (platform.twitter.com, x.com and X's CDNs) and X may set cookies in this app's own private browser profile (not your normal browser profile). slopTV asks X not to personalise from your activity (dnt=true), but it does not control what X's player does.

Autoplay may need one click on the video.

Keys

l / → next   h / ← previous   s shuffle   a auto-advance on/off   p / Space pause and resume (freezes the countdown, shows larger previews)   f like (opens X's like page, you confirm there)   o open post on X   ? help   q quit

Auto-advance is timer-based. X's player lives in a cross-origin iframe and slopTV does not touch it, so it cannot know when the video ends. It moves on after the post's known duration (plus a second). Clicking inside the player pauses the countdown (we cannot see X's own pause button); press p / Space or click this bar to resume from the remaining time. Posts with no known duration stay until you press next. After the last post, next wraps to the first.

Keys work when this bottom bar (not the video) has focus: click it, or press Tab. Once you click inside X's player, the player keeps the keyboard.
